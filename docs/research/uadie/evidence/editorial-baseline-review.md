# Independent editorial review of the UADIE publication baseline

**Decision at the source cut.** Hold public release of both final editions. The reviewed source tree at base e29c62e contains the research materials and publication scripts, but neither requested book manuscript exists at the cut. Diagnostic PDF and Word files generated for this review contain a clearly synthetic acceptance fixture. They cannot be substituted for either book. The editorial reviewer worked in an isolated copy and did not edit the shared worktree.

## What the measurements can support

Report 3,360 historical rows across stages, including repeated arms, rotations and base/revised models. The final 2,016 rows came from 56 pairs, six arms, two revisions and three rotations. Accepted transport counts use revised instances as denominator and rotations must be collapsed by case, not treated as independent. The prior raw timing edition reports warm 21.73 μs, full 30.53 μs, pair 57.91 μs, box 70.38 μs, reuse 73.91 μs and partition 79.22 μs. Pair closed more certificates than box (22/56 versus 14/56). The 0/8 versus 8/8 common-shift result applies specifically to that family. Warm had the lowest aggregate median. The shortage-family medians show that this ranking does not apply to every family. The old split became development evidence when those outcomes were inspected.

The separate native comparator reported 656 model records, including repeated base revisions, evaluated by six candidates to yield 3,936 comparisons and zero disagreements in that comparison. 112 counts infeasible model records. Its native runtime was not part of the matched historical timing experiment. Do not pool the 5,265 mathematical truth-table cases with the 426,465 engineering inequalities into an independent sample size. PPV/error-rate estimates on these deterministic exhaustions have no usual sampling interpretation. Report proof assumptions, executed finite checks, solver adjudication and release qualification separately.

## Reproduction and final-read protocol

Run the original publication tools on the private fixture tree with PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 corpus/tools/publish.py. The tiny input manuscripts and corpus/diagrams/qa.svg are deliberate. Render pages 3 and 4 with the local poppler-utils tools; inspect the source fixture beside the output. Check the explicit Arabic token, the displayed equation, three numbered steps, the closed static table, the ordinary relative link and the grouped green “VISIBLE NODE” figure. No edit to the original research files is required.

For a final edition freeze Markdown, figure sources, SVGs, results tables, source commit and renderer version first. Verify PDF links and text extraction. Render every native PDF page and separately render the complete DOCX with a genuine Word-compatible layout engine; review page numbers, labels, diagrams, tables, RTL, continuity, cropped text and blank pages. Validate scientific claims at their source and cross-check a worked example by hand. Record page-level observations and exact hashes. The sampled PDF screenshots in this review establish what two fixture pages looked like, and cannot attest to the layout of a future manuscript. Independent human peer review remains outstanding.
