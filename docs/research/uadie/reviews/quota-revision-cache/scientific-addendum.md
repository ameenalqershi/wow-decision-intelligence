# UADIE research supplement — normalized quota residual and exact structure reuse
Status: review addendum, not a replacement of the pinned scientific manuscript.
## Mathematical scope
Let binary x satisfy x_i+x_j=1 on a graph, and one exact quota sum over S of x_i=k. Minimize signed integer residual sum d_i*x_i. Other constraints are omitted only to construct a lower bound. Bipartite components have two orientations. Let counts a_j<=b_j and costs A_j,B_j correspond to those orientations; w_j=b_j-a_j, v_j=B_j-A_j. Equal counts can be folded into a constant with min(A_j,B_j). The remaining low-count orientation contributes its cost to constant C. Subtract all low counts from k to obtain T. Feasible assignments correspond to binary y_j satisfying sum w_j*y_j=T and have minimum residual C+min sum v_j*y_j. This is exact for the stated relaxation. If T is outside [0,sum w], or not divisible by gcd(w), infeasibility follows. GCD divisibility is necessary but not sufficient. Divide all weights and T by the gcd. Complement all selections if T exceeds half their sum; update C by sum v_j and negate each v_j. If all resulting weights are one, take the T smallest signed differences; otherwise descend over states q in [0,T] for each component, with D[0]=0 and infinity elsewhere, updating D[q]=min(D[q],D[q-w_j]+v_j) only from finite previous states. Descending order ensures one use of each component. Return null for unreachable T. Magnitude envelope sum abs(d)<Int64.MaxValue reserves infinity and bounds partial sums of disjoint original terms and orientation differences. Nonempty domain required. No weighted quotas, inequalities, nonbinary variables or multiple quotas qualified.
## Revision-safe structure reuse
Cache copies the exact edge sequence, membership bits, count and variable count. Admission compares these exact values, not merely a hash. If they differ, rebuild a complete plan; if equal, reuse the component partition and normalized structural metadata only. Fresh d is checked and all costs recomputed on every call. Correctness follows from exact structure equality and the preceding reduction. Edges may be reordered or duplicated: conservative invalidation is safe. Caller owns arrays without mutation during the call; concurrent usage, cross-tenant identity, resource accounting and persistent cache are not qualified. Cancellation checks do not guarantee a bounded response during sorting/graph construction.
