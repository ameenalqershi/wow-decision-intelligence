# G7 bounded adaptive certificate policy: results
Frozen source/protocol a4d3ea4. Development 3,360/3,360 valid timing records (60 histories), holdout 13,440/13,440 (240 histories); seven arms each, eight revisions per history, same exact exhaustive original-model oracle. Not independent samples across arms or repeated seeds. Holdout adaptive: 585/1,680 later revisions transported (34.82%), 585/1,920 all decisions (30.47%). 1,335/1,920 required exact solve (including initial revisions); 1,246 certificate attempts; 285 identity/restriction, 100 Box, 143 parity, 57 normalized quota successful. 38 quota cache hits (not all cache calls counted, no hit ratio claimed). Hierarchy transported 1,044/1,680 (62.14%), with 2,891 attempts; more avoided solves did not mean lower total latency.
Holdout adaptive median 38.070 us, p90 62.562, p95 72.974, p99 204.633. Warm median 30.955 us, p95 132.723; orientation median 38.059 us, p95 62.800. Paired full-history median speedup adaptive vs full 1.011 (121/240 wins), warm 0.787 (76/240), orientation 0.952 (103/240), parity-warm 0.931, component-reuse 1.965, unconditional hierarchy 1.113. Exploratory seed-cluster bootstrap CI against warm [0.752,0.817] and hierarchy [1.078,1.146]; vs full [0.949,1.106]. Seed-cluster median is a separate estimand, summary reports both. No multiple-comparison correction. No general performance promotion. The empirical selection proxy and gating are rejected as a universal default; retain as research challenger. Important: shared timed pipeline includes original witness checking and memory evidence encoding, but not independent exhaustive proof checking, disk durability, or production compiler/runtime. All reference optimality checks occur outside timings. Per-process peak RSS holdout 65,792 KiB, 3.04s wall, 2.85s user CPU, 0.19s system CPU; no arm-specific RSS attribution. The result supports limiting certificate cascades, not using a learned policy in production.
