# Comparative method register

**Review basis.** Targeted original papers, author manuscripts and official solver reports, reviewed 28 November 2026. The scientific judgments in the application column are the reviewer's synthesis. The selection emphasizes close competitors to revision-aware reuse. It is a technical literature search, with incomplete coverage of unpublished results and patent records. For the project's pre-existing comparison and hypotheses see [scientific map](../../methods.md) and [innovation register](../../innovation.md).

**Reading the cost column.** Let n be the number of decisions, d the maximum finite domain size, L the input bit length, I the total factor incidences, S the number of scenarios, and T the number of search nodes actually visited. Polynomial bounds below refer to the specified subproblem. Each proposed integration needs a capability declaration and a source-level evidence check. All attributions in this table describe prior art.

| Method and decision problem | Mechanism, mathematical basis and advantage | Cost driver, success condition and failure mode | Design consequence for WOW DI and comparator |
|---|---|---|---|
| LP, continuous linear allocation | Feasible primal and dual values sandwich the optimum. An exact feasible pair with equal values closes the gap. | Polynomial bit-complexity algorithms exist for rational LP. Simplex can visit exponentially many bases. Fill-in and conditioning dominate many numerical runs. | Verify rational witnesses or classify numerical residuals explicitly. Compare full solve and basis reuse. [Exact rational solver report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18580) |
| MILP, discrete setup and continuous allocation | Restrict selected LP coordinates to integers. Relaxation lower bounds prune subtrees in minimization. | General NP-hard optimization. Weak big-M coefficients permit very loose relaxations. Polynomial LP solvability alone gives no integer optimality proof. | Admit typed MIP lowering and distinguish relaxation, feasible incumbent and closed gap. [Land and Doig](https://jmvidal.cse.sc.edu/library/land60a.pdf) |
| Branch and bound | Partition a feasible search region exhaustively into cases. A node lower bound exceeding an incumbent eliminates that node for minimization. | Up to exponential T. At fixed T costs include node processing, bound derivation and ordering. Equality pruning may discard tied solutions when all ties are requested. | Require a covered tree or another globally validated bound. Revalidate every pruning premise across revisions. [Land and Doig](https://jmvidal.cse.sc.edu/library/land60a.pdf) |
| Cutting planes | Add inequalities implied for all integer feasible points, potentially cutting the fractional LP optimum. | Cut discovery, separation and repeated LP solves can dominate. Incorrect rounding or integrality assumptions remove feasible points. | Scope cuts to exact premises and validate the derivation before cross-revision reuse. [Gomory](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~rgomory/academic_papers/07_outline.pdf) |
| Branch and cut | Interleave valid inequalities with the search tree. Improved bounds can shrink T. | No polynomial guarantee for general MIP. Local cuts depend on node assumptions. | Include assumptions in proofs and segregate globally valid cuts from locally valid cuts. [SCIP 10](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18580) |
| Constraint programming | Propagate necessary consequences of finite-domain constraints and branch until a full assignment or contradiction. | Up to dⁿ assignments. Stronger propagators can cost more per node. A fixed point can retain an unsatisfiable instance. | Compare against scheduling CP on eligible UDIR operators. Check the original global constraints. [Mackworth](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~mack/Publications/AI77.pdf) |
| Incremental constraint propagation | Enqueue incident constraints after a value loses support. Monotone domain contraction on finite domains reaches a fixed point. | Total cost depends on deletions, incidence and support search. Relaxing constraints requires restoring potentially lost supports. | Attach reasons to removals or recompute the affected closure from original domains. [Consistency in networks of relations](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~mack/Publications/AI77.pdf) |
| SAT | Express decisions as Boolean clauses. Branching and learned consequences reduce repeated conflict exploration. | Boolean satisfiability is NP-complete. Encodings can inflate the number of variables. Wrong activation scopes make learned clauses unsound. | Include assumption literals and encoding equivalence in a replayable proof. [SATzilla's SAT solver comparisons](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2249) |
| SMT | Combine Boolean search with theory consistency, for example linear arithmetic. | Depends on logic fragment. Some theory combinations or quantified problems lack a complete terminating procedure. | Declare supported theories. Preserve unknown as a terminal result of a bounded run. [de Moura and Bjørner](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/z3-an-efficient-smt-solver/) |
| Benders decomposition | Fix linking decisions. Solve conditional recourse and derive feasibility or optimality cuts for the master. | Repeated master and subproblem solves. Classical LP dual cuts depend on recourse structure and valid ray handling. Integer recourse needs separate arguments. | Competing strategy for linked blocks. Independent concatenation would omit the master consistency conditions. [Benders](https://www.im-uff.mat.br/puc-rio/disciplinas/2006.1/soe/arquivos/benders-numerische-mathematik-1962.pdf) |
| Dantzig–Wolfe | Represent block polyhedra via extreme points and rays. The master enforces common linking rows. | Potentially exponentially many columns. Unbounded block polyhedra may require rays. | Candidate for block-angular linear models with linking rows. An integer master restricted to generated LP columns may miss the IP optimum. [Dantzig and Wolfe](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.8.1.101) |
| Column generation | Iterate between a restricted master and pricing over omitted patterns. Reduced costs determine optimality for the full LP. | Pricing can itself be NP-hard. A heuristic pricing failure cannot establish dual feasibility over all patterns. | Retain valid reduced-cost or exact pricing certificates before accepting a master LP bound. [Gilmore and Gomory](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.9.6.849) |
| Lagrangian relaxation | Penalize linked inequalities with correctly signed multipliers. An exact relaxed minimization gives a lower bound. | Repeat subsolves per multiplier update. Integer duality gaps can remain even at optimal multipliers. | Use as a lower-bound provider. Verify separately any assembled primal solution. [Fisher](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.27.1.1) |
| Dynamic programming | A sufficient state summarizes history. Bellman recurrence optimizes one decision plus the cost-to-go. | Time proportional to visited state–action transitions and transition cost. Resource dimensions or horizon can cause state explosion. | A bounded independent oracle if the state records every future-relevant quantity. If setup order matters, inventory alone is insufficient. [Bellman](https://www.derongliu.org/adp/adp-cdrom/refs/bellman19520716.pdf) |
| Graph partitioning | Identify connected components of the complete undirected factor-incidence representation. Balanced cut heuristics instead partition a connected graph. | Components take linear graph time. A small nonempty cut still couples the resulting blocks. | Count and inspect all crossing terms. A cheap cut motivates conditional decomposition with a separator. [Constraint optimization decomposition](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~fbacchus/Papers/MB_CP2008.pdf) |
| Hypergraph partitioning | Represent a joint term using one hyperedge incident to every argument. Optimize connectivity or cut metrics when balancing blocks. | Linear traversal in the incidence representation suffices for zero-crossing components. Balanced minimum-cut partitioning can be NP-hard. | A hypergraph partitioner can propose boundaries. Exact reuse requires checking all crossing rows, costs and global policies. [Scalable high-quality hypergraph partitioning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17679) |
| Strongly connected components | Group vertices mutually reachable in directed dependencies. The condensation graph has a topological order. | O(vertices + arcs) with adjacency lists. An arc between two SCCs represents dependence in one direction. | Use for scheduling recomputation. Read directed dependencies separately from undirected mathematical coupling. [Tarjan](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cdm/resources/Tarjan1972-sccs.pdf) |
| Incremental solving | Revisit saved search regions after objective changes or restrictions. Some previously discarded regions can be relevant again. | Tree retention can approach the full search size. Objective-dependent pruning can expire. | Compare against a native reoptimizing full solver with the same historical access. Scope each preserved proof to the admissible change class. [Reoptimization techniques](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/4898.pdf) |
| Warm starting | Seed a compatible basis, feasible incumbent, or solver hint. Re-evaluate the previous decision with the new coefficients. | Reading a dense incumbent costs Ω(n). Setup, remapping and repairs can consume the whole benefit. | Treat as budgeted input to a new run. A primal hint alone cannot certify optimality. [Native MIP reoptimization](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/4898.pdf) |
| Dynamic and online optimization | Optimize actions as requests arrive. Compare cost against a causal baseline or an explicitly clairvoyant reference. | Horizon, state, switching costs and arrival model determine any competitive guarantee. Reoptimizing an offline suffix can conflict with executed actions. | Freeze already executed actions in the revised feasible set. Evaluate on ordered streams with identical information arrival. [Sleator and Tarjan](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sleator/papers/Amortized-Efficiency.htm) |
| Robust optimization | Enforce constraints over every uncertainty realization in a declared set. Budgeted uncertainty controls conservatism. | Complexity depends on the support function and integrality. Changing uncertainty correlations changes feasible decisions. | Include the set definition and joint budget in dependency and equality checks. [Bertsimas and Sim](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.1030.0065) |
| Stochastic programming | Shared here-and-now decisions and scenario-specific recourse optimize expected cost under explicit probabilities. | Explicit deterministic equivalents replicate recourse across S scenarios. Rare events and scenario reduction can change conclusions. | Preserve nonanticipativity links and version scenario weights. Optimize the specified finite model before making out-of-sample claims. [Dantzig](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.1.3-4.197) |
| Multi-objective optimization | Lexicographic order, weighted scalarization and Pareto enumeration answer different questions. | Sequential lexicographic solving can require one exact solve per priority. The Pareto frontier can be exponentially large. | Cache priority and tolerance semantics. Scalar optima do not establish the full frontier. [Biobjective branch and bound](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/ijoc.2021.1092) |
| Solver portfolios | Run eligible strategies with a specified temporal or parallel allocation. | Startup and feature extraction consume budget. Parallel arms compete for cores and RAM. | Account for total CPU and wall time. Compare to the single best eligible solver and a static schedule. [SATzilla](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2249) |
| Algorithm selection | Predict the strongest eligible solver for an instance from cheaply available features. | Inference and feature costs plus selected algorithm. Data leakage between near-identical revisions can exaggerate benefit. | Freeze family-level splits and report feature failures. The virtual best solver is an oracle reference. [ASlib](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02465) |
| Algorithm configuration | Search parameter settings offline using repeatable training evaluations. | Trial count multiplied by evaluation budget. Adaptive capping changes the interpretation of incomplete runs. | Freeze configuration before final validation and account for tuning cost separately. [ParamILS](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.3492) |
| Proof-carrying optimization | A solver emits checkable derivations of infeasibility or bounds plus primal witnesses when applicable. | Cost follows proof length, coefficient bit sizes and checker rules. Proof logging, storing, parsing and checking can dominate solution time. | Bind the exact source model to the proved IR. Treat proof transport as a separately checkable derivation. [CP 2025 pseudo-Boolean proof logging](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2025.21) |
| Memoization and safe result reuse | Track dynamic dependencies and validate memoized results at their current inputs. | Work ranges from lookup to full recomputation plus invalidation. A stale dependency can make a cache hit wrong. | Separate equality of canonical payloads from evidence type, trust scope and retrieval eligibility. [Self-adjusting computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0478) |
| Learning-augmented algorithms | A prediction initializes a primal–dual algorithm, which repairs it and still checks its mathematical postconditions. | Theoretical gains are problem- and error-model-specific. Out-of-distribution errors may consume repair time. | Evaluate predictive ordering only behind a deterministic cap. Matching guarantees require a matching formulation. [Dinitz et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.09770) |

## Direct competitors and qualification decisions

1. Reoptimization of an entire branch-and-bound tree can retain information across objective changes and domain restrictions even with a single connected component. Beating cold enumeration leaves this comparison open. Measure the same sequence under native reoptimization, with warm-state memory and startup charged. [Native reoptimization study](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/4898.pdf)
2. Component caching predates WOW DI. Even exact separation can weaken bound propagation across search. An implementation-specific benefit would be lower end-to-end cost for selected revision sequences while carrying source-scoped certificates. [Kitching and Bacchus](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~fbacchus/Papers/MB_CP2008.pdf)
3. Current exact MIP tooling can emit independently checkable evidence. The 2026 LeanVIPR workshop manuscript describes a sound, terminating checker for parsed VIPR input. The model-to-input mapping still needs an independent check. Qualification should compare proof generation and replay costs, with the original rational model retained. [SCIP 10](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18580) · [LeanVIPR manuscript](https://hocdot.github.io/assets/papers/song2026leanvipr.pdf)
4. The project-specific idea worth testing is composition of artifact-specific transport, revision-scoped mathematical identity, conservative impact and a budgeted fallback. None of the component theorems establishes that the combination saves resources across sectors. A final scientific priority or patent determination remains open.
